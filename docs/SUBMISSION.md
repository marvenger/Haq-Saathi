# Submission pack

## 1. Explain your solution (form limit 500 characters, this text is 453)

Haq Saathi is a voice-first Hindi/English app that makes investor protection usable for first-time users in Tier-2/3 India. Scam Shield flags fraud messages and fake recovery agents on-device. A family claim guide lists documents and steps after a death. A complaint assistant drafts a broker/DP complaint with the SCORES and Smart ODR path. A nominee tracker shows unprotected accounts. No stock tips, no tracking, no PAN/OTP/SMS access. Works offline.
